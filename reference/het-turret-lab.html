Against hull

Full hull output once shields are down, all barrels counted. Weapons tagged pierce land that share of this damage on hull even while shields are still up. Hull damage is never reduced by enemy resistances — those live on the shield.

Against shields

Weapons with a shield multiplier of zero are absent here — Flamethrower, Salvaging Laser and both Hammer Heads cannot damage shields at all. Only Physical, Plasma, Electric and AntiMatter can be resisted; Energy and Fragments always land in full.

Loadout solver

Picks integer turret counts for an armed-slot budget — Unarmed (Salvaging, Mining, Hammer Heads) and Defense (A.D.S.T., Anti-Fighter, Torpedo) turrets mount on their own slot pools and never compete here; stack salvagers on top of any solution for free hull DPS. Objective blends time-to-kill across a range band with the worst case if the target's shield resists one damage type at 95% (hull damage is never resisted, and Energy / Fragments weapons can't be resisted at all). Reserve a fixed count of any armed turret to guarantee it survives the solve — useful for keeping knife-range Flamethrowers the range band would otherwise cut.

Values are peak effective DPS divided by turret slots, at the point-blank end of each weapon's envelope; the charts show decay across range. Every figure sums the turret's real barrel assembly from turretgenerator.lua — Predator ×6 (asymmetric: barrel 1 uses 600+t, the rest 20+t), Proton ×7, Gatling ×5, Clandatoh / Interceptor / A.D.S.T. ×4, Ophidian 3×SW + 1×CW (the central barrel carries hull ×15), Triad / Flamethrower ×3, APCR ×2 (the generator re-adds its weapon without clearing). Flight-time penalty is modelled as 1/(1+t) — beams and seekers take none. Duty cycle uses each turret's real shootingTime/(shootingTime+coolingTime); the Pulse Shotgun pre-compensates its own duty in code, and the Flamethrower's is rolled per turret (10–45 s shooting / 17.5–43.75 s cooling). Factory investment applies stat × (1 + ΣinvestFactor). Enemy resistance is Shield:setResistance — shield-only, ~95%, one of Physical / Plasma / Electric / AntiMatter on ~half of ships at Normal; hull damage and all Energy / Fragments weapons are immune. Slot classes follow weapontype.lua: Salvaging / Mining / Hammer Heads are Unarmed, A.D.S.T. / Anti-Fighter / Torpedo are Defense. The HET Salvaging Laser calls adaptWeapon (full ×(1+0.4·rarity)), not the mining ×1.05 path, on a fireDelay of 0.02 — a genuine outlier, but it rides free on unarmed slots. Clandatoh range is capped by projectile lifetime (pmaximumTime is never rescaled), so its card range overstates reality. Flamethrower shield penetration at Exceptional is 0.8. Budget constant fitted to 0.4439 against in-game cards (tech 36: Ophidian, Predator ×3 rarities, Clandatoh, Flamethrower, Salvaging ×2); difficulty does not scale turret stats; sector ship volume uses the mod's override of Balancing_GetSectorShipVolume. The game's own per-type eDPS card lines are built from the first weapon only and can misreport multi-barrel turrets — trust the Raw DPS field.
